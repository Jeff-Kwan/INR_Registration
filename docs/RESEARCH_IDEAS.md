# Registration research ideas

The [README](../README.md) lists the current shared 5k recipe. This notebook keeps observations worth following up, including gains confined to one case, motion range, or dataset. For each idea, explain what could produce the observation, how the proposed intervention acts on it, and which result would make us reconsider.

## Live mechanisms from the ten-hour session

**Optimize common anatomy and endpoint difference in separate Adam coordinates — tested, no shared gain.** The endpoint pair contains a common image component and a component that creates temporal change. Independent endpoint Adam mixes both gradients before estimating their second moments. If common reconstruction gradients dominate that estimate, useful difference updates may be suppressed; if the difference is mostly noisy, separating it could instead amplify that noise. Probe weighted gradients in `m=(source+target)/2`, `d=(target-source)/2` coordinates, then compare Adam in those coordinates against the same objective and endpoint-space clipping. The chain-rule gradients are `g_m=g_source+g_target`, `g_d=g_target-g_source`; after updates recover endpoints as `m-d`, `m+d`. Weight decay commutes with this linear transformation. A matched SGD control with half the transformed-coordinate LR checks algebra independently of Adam's coordinate dependence. Judge actual endpoint update sizes, transport consistency, both-direction TRE, transfer and folding. Improvement without a corresponding increase in endpoint update magnitude would strengthen the preconditioning explanation.

**Anatomy-first training can establish an unhelpful correspondence — preliminary negative result.** A1000-step shared random-endpoint reconstruction prefix gives better5k endpoint-window NCC than the joint start(0.853 versus0.827), but worse DIR-Lab forward TRE(2.576 versus1.797mm). Its large-motion hidden-feature correspondence margins and temporal-target correlations are lower. Better endpoint fits alone do not guarantee a useful parameter path. The independent-prefix control separates delayed registration from the common fit. A sharp single-endpoint prefix remains a possible test of blur versus identity locking; it should follow the control's result, with directional bias reported.

The common/difference Adam trial finished all three frozen quick4 sets: DIR-Lab forward TRE2.030mm, OASIS Dice0.82589, abdomen Dice0.41367. Initial common gradients were9–22times larger than difference gradients, but naive separation did not help registration. The independent1000-step reconstruction prefix also worsened DIR-Lab TRE to2.207mm. Delaying registration is harmful even without shared-image blur; improved reconstruction by itself is an insufficient criterion.

**Match hidden phase updates when changing width — tested local control.** A first Adam step atLR1e-4 moves hidden phases by roughly0.067rad atwidth256,0.10at384 and0.13at512, while the first-layer phase stays near0.0055rad. Width therefore changes the optimizer's functional step as well as capacity. Compare384 atits usualLR against1.3times hidden/headLR while preserving first-layerLR. Follow with256 at2times hidden/headLR if the first control supports this explanation. Charge recovery cost to the comparison. Matching the initial phase is a local control; it does not establish equal trajectories throughout training.

**Bend the path while freezing endpoint fidelity — tested, small effect.** On case08,5k→40k raises endpoint-window NCC0.663→0.798 but forwardTREonly8.480→7.431mm; temporal-target alignment stops improving afterabout20k. A linear path through two networks may be unable to express a useful intermediate image sequence even with better endpoints. Freeze both well-fitted SIRENs and add4t(1−t)times a learned deviation to the first hidden weight/bias block. Its temporal derivative uses4(1−2t); output and first-layer blocks stay linear. Endpoint predictions remain exactly unchanged. Better TRE would isolate a parameter-path limitation; collapsing intermediate contrast or worse folding would expose an unhelpful shortcut. Exact analytical values and full parameterVJPs pass an autograd check, and repair-only updates leave the endpoints bitwise unchanged.

## Learn coarse displacement first, then refine local alignment

A vessel can be farther from its counterpart than the width of its local intensity profile. In that situation an image-similarity loss can have a sharp peak at the right alignment yet provide little directional signal at the starting position. Broad anatomical gradients could give the optimizer a direction while the mismatch is large. Once the vessel is close, its sharp boundaries could supply the precision needed to finish alignment. This explanation predicts different requirements for *finding* a correspondence and *refining* it.

On DIR-Lab quick4, we linearly shrank the shared crop from 0.75 to 0.25 of each image dimension over 5k steps. The control held it at 0.50. Both used the recommended width-512 recipe and seed 42.

| Measure | Fixed 0.50 | Shrinking crop |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Forward / reverse TRE (mm) | 1.797 / 1.832 | 1.801 / 1.878 |
| Forward TRE for motion ≥10 mm (mm) | 2.860 | 2.722 |
| Forward TRE for motion <2 mm (mm) | 0.953 | 1.045 |
| Endpoint landmark patch NCC | 0.827 | 0.819 |

Case 05 accounts for almost the entire pooled large-motion gain. Its ≥10 mm TRE fell from 3.999 to 3.467 mm. Small-motion TRE worsened in all four cases. With a fixed 8³ velocity grid, changing crop size also changes point density, cell spacing and the physical smoothing bandwidth. [Experiment preset](../medical/configs/ablations/native_siren_crop_075_to_025.json) · [Local paired results](../results/dirlab_crop_075_to_025_quick4/comparison.json)

The intended curriculum is to establish displacement amplitude from broad anatomical structures early, then refine local alignment against fine detail. The pilot varied the spatial extent of the velocity grid while using full-resolution image evidence throughout. Small displacements can occur inside any crop. One possible explanation for their regression is that the schedule reached its finest grid only at the end of OneCycle, when the learning rate was already near its minimum.

Sampling also changed substantially. A 0.75-width box has about 27 times the volume of a 0.25-width box. With the same point count, the early box has much sparser samples per voxel, while the late box covers fewer anatomical regions per update. Measure points per physical volume and cumulative regional exposure alongside grid cell width. Landmark coverage and motion bins can be measured during evaluation.

**Probe the image signal first.** On the observed image pair, evaluate patch NCC at offsets along known landmark displacements, with and without Gaussian smoothing in millimetres. For large motions, does a small step from the starting position toward the true match improve NCC more consistently after smoothing? Near the true match, does full-resolution NCC localize the peak more precisely? This evaluation-only probe isolates the image evidence from velocity-grid spacing and training dynamics. Smoothing can also erase a distinctive vessel. A flatter curve with no more reliable direction would weaken the rationale for blurred supervision.

**Let the probe determine the training test.** If smoothing extends the range of useful directions, apply it early to the images used by registration NCC, then restore full detail for refinement. A fixed crop isolates this change in image evidence. The shorter refinement-budget hypothesis makes a different prediction: after the same coarse training prefix, continuing at fine scale should reduce small-motion TRE while preserving the amplitude already recovered. Compare checkpoint trajectories against an equal-length continuation at the previous scale, with the same learning-rate history. The final scores from the shrinking-crop pilot alone cannot distinguish these explanations.

**Can coarse and fine evidence cooperate within a step?** A broad image feature may supply a direction over a long displacement, while fine texture localizes a nearby match precisely. At a frozen field, compute registration-NCC gradients for a blurred and an original image on the *same* particles. Use known landmark displacements only in evaluation to check whether coarse guidance points toward the match at large offsets and fine guidance remains informative near alignment. If that separation appears, test retaining coarse guidance while introducing the fine term. Match the overall update scale and compare both motion bins and their signed errors. If both scales point the same way or both lose direction at large offsets, blending them has little mechanistic motivation.

## Does each update carry reproducible motion information?

**Gradient noise and objective conflict predict different failures.** Freeze an early, middle and late model. At each, repeat independent training draws and collect the weighted gradients of endpoint reconstruction (including moments), PD-LK and registration NCC on the same draw. For each term and layer, compare the norm of the mean gradient with the RMS deviation across draws. Measure pairwise gradient cosines and the norm of the summed gradient relative to the sum of individual norms. Repeat with the crop fixed and resample only particles or probe directions to locate the source of variability. Use disjoint draws to check whether a proposed step decreases its intended objective. A reproducible gradient can still favour an inaccurate motion field, so check recovered TRE and transport alignment separately.

If the velocity probes dominate variance, spend the next compute on improving their spatial coverage at equal sample count. If repeatable reconstruction and registration gradients oppose each other early and align later, delaying registration has a specific rationale. An algebraic rearrangement with identical samples and exact gradients predicts identical updates in exact arithmetic. Any claimed benefit from rearrangement alone calls for a numerical parity check.

**When does anatomy make registration gradients informative?** Immediately after copying one SIREN into two, the interpolated image is constant in time and its temporal derivative is zero, even for a well-fitted shared image. The prediction concerns the first steps *after* the endpoints diverge. On fixed evaluation coordinates track endpoint gradient structure, reproducibility of registration gradients, temporal-target alignment and large-motion TRE through this transition. Compare a shared reconstruction prefix with two independently fitting endpoints over an equally long reconstruction-only prefix, as well as the existing joint start. Charge each prefix to elapsed time. This separates the benefit of giving anatomy time to emerge from the benefit of retaining a shared parameter representation.

## Which SIREN updates limit convergence?

- **Hidden-layer phase updates — one-step mechanism demonstrated.** A hidden unit responds to a change in the phase of its sine. Multiplying that phase by ω changes how far the function moves for a given weight update. With identical initial predictions and derivatives, hidden ω=15 with doubled hidden parameters matched ω=30 with half the hidden learning rate after one update. Repeat that matched comparison over 5k steps, recording phase changes on the same coordinates at each milestone. Measure the RMS change in hidden preactivation phase per update on fixed coordinates alongside held-out loss change, temporal alignment and TRE. Try both a smaller and a larger hidden-layer update around the reference with the same initial function. Larger movement could accelerate a coherent update or disrupt useful spatial features. Use time to a common registration quality to decide which effect dominates and whether it changes during training. [Controlled probe](SIREN_CONVERGENCE.md#follow-up-hidden-omega-mechanism-probe)
- **Output initialization and motion along the parameter path — tested signal.** Endpoint supervision constrains two images. Recovery also depends on how intensities change between them as their parameters interpolate. Standard head initialization raised endpoint NCC from 0.825 to 0.843, yet large-motion TRE rose from 3.044 to 4.194 mm. Endpoint windows at true correspondences had nearly identical correlation. Probe the saved early checkpoints to locate when the temporal derivative first departs from the brightness-flow target along observed landmark paths. A discrepancy early in training would motivate testing the timing of registration supervision. Landmarks serve only as evaluation sites. [Output control](SIREN_CONVERGENCE.md#output-initialization-control-and-intermediate-transport)
- **Capacity under a wall-clock budget.** Extra features could fit fine structure in fewer updates. A smaller network could instead benefit from more updates before the same deadline. Compare time to recovered registration quality across widths, including the dense recovery cost. [Performance investigation](PERFORMANCE_RESEARCH.md)
- **Learn a common image representation before splitting the endpoints — proposed.** Both SIRENs begin as copies of the same random function. A reconstruction prefix could give corresponding channels shared anatomical features before endpoint specialization. That might make parameter interpolation produce a more coherent temporal derivative. A single-image prefix could favour its direction, while drawing equally from both unregistered images could average structures across their displacement. At the split, measure sharpness and endpoint fidelity. Immediately afterwards, measure how quickly the temporal derivative aligns with the observed motion and how forward/reverse errors evolve. Compare to the direct paired start with pretraining and recovery charged to the same total time. Keep evaluation landmarks out of pretraining and stopping. If sharper features fail to improve the intermediate path, reconsider shared reconstruction as the limiting step.

## Which spatial scales transfer?

- **Input frequencies in physical units — proposed mechanism test.** First-layer weights moved only about 2–3% over 5k steps. The same initial ω therefore leaves a persistent spatial scale whose wavelength in millimetres depends on image extent. The 20–30 ladder raised abdomen Dice from 0.41690 to 0.43098, with a small OASIS decline. Measure first-layer phase changes over identical physical offsets across datasets, then compare a physically matched input initialization with uniform 30 on all three frozen sets. Keep native image sampling and velocity units fixed. [Frequency transfer and geometry](SIREN_CONVERGENCE.md#frequency-transfer-and-image-geometry)
- **Structure-centred crops — mixed pilot evidence.** A small boundary may supply a strong spatial gradient yet appear in few random crops. Increasing its exposure could help if infrequent supervision limits alignment. Global structure-weighted endpoint sampling improved OASIS Dice over uniform global sampling on all four pairs. A separate 50/50 mixture of ordinary and structure-centred shared crops reached 0.81819 Dice. Compare the direct-centering mixture against ordinary crops under the same shared 5k recipe. Measure small-label Dice and within-label patch NCC to see whether extra exposure improves alignment as well as reconstruction. Use image gradients for training and labels for evaluation. [Sampling methods and controls](STRUCTURE_SAMPLING.md)
- **Velocity grid spacing at fixed coverage — unresolved.** A single velocity cell pools image evidence from neighbouring locations. If their motions differ, a locally averaged velocity can underestimate motion even when the image contains a clear match. With the same fractional crop and 8³ grid, larger DIR-Lab cases can have roughly 31 mm in-plane cells versus 15–18 mm on smaller cases. Earlier physical-grid trials changed several settings together. Vary grid resolution on the native images while holding crop coverage and physical smoothing bandwidth fixed. Compare along-motion and perpendicular errors to see which component responds. ## Where do the objectives supply motion information?

- **Two orthogonal probe directions per cell — measured useful lead.** Nearly parallel random offsets can collect very similar evidence twice. Sampling a uniformly rotated orthonormal two-frame in physical space preserves isotropy across draws and spreads the two symmetric pairs within a cell. Image gradients at those four sites can still be parallel along the same anatomical edge. On frozen fields, hold the cell centre, physical offset radius, evaluation count and regularization fixed. Compare repeat-draw velocity variance, local normal-matrix conditioning and velocity error against a densely sampled cell estimate. Separate textured regions from single edges and flat regions. Advance to a training comparison if geometry improves velocity accuracy or repeatability in cells carrying motion information. - **PD-LK with weak registration supervision — dataset-dependent signal.** The brightness-flow relation constrains the velocity component along an image gradient. With endpoint fitting active and registration NCC weight zero, six-layer PD-LK at weight 0.05 reached 0.8225 OASIS Dice. ## Route anatomical change through parameters that can express motion

**A coherent translation optimizer mode — tested, no broad gain.** If the first-layer frequencies and later layers are fixed, shifting the first-layer bias by the input matrix times a coordinate translation shifts the entire SIREN exactly. Linear endpoint bias interpolation then represents a finite translation exactly. Baseline first-layer phase updates are much smaller than hidden-matrix updates, despite this useful geometric route. Separate the joint first-bias gradient into a three-dimensional relative-translation component and its orthogonal residual. Optimize the coherent component with a1mm peak Adam step, retaining ordinary AdamW for all other directions and the same OneCycle multiplier. This changes optimizer geometry without adding a deformation predictor or changing checkpoint/recovery semantics. The exact-translation and gradient-projection identities pass synthetic checks;600 changing-crop captured updates pass. Observe inferred motion amplitude, both TRE directions and early objective gradients before changing the mode's step scale.

**Paired sine/cosine input features — tested, no shared gain.** Translating a sine produces a sine/cosine combination. Explicit quadrature pairs let the first hidden matrix express the translation tangent while input frequencies remain fixed. The corresponding hidden-weight rotation formula matches the analytical translation derivative to about1.3e-7 relative error at initialization; the same formula on random unpaired features has order-one error. Training can break the initial quadrature relationship, and pairing halves the number of distinct input wavevectors, so this is a representation tradeoff rather than an automatic gain. Compare endpoint fidelity and transport alignment separately.

**Shared weight matrices with separate bias codes — tested, too restrictive for CT.** A reconstruction prefix gave the pair an identity correspondence before registration could influence it. A different possibility is to keep the anatomical matrices shared throughout joint training and let interpolated bias codes carry temporal change. This retains two endpoint SIRENs and the same analytic velocity/recovery. It removes independent endpoint matrix drift and makes the temporal derivative cheaper because all matrix directions are identically zero. Bias phases otherwise move far less per Adam step than hidden-matrix phases. Compare the ordinary bias learning rate with a30times sine-bias rate, corresponding to roughly0.09rad at the peak; keep output-bias updates ordinary to preserve the intensity-gauge control. Exact g,q,H,m and full shared-parameter VJPs match the general derivative chain. If fidelity falls while transport improves, test capacity before concluding that shared anatomy is unhelpful. If this helps, an independent-matrix bias-rate control is required to isolate the contribution of sharing.

**Endpoint mismatch lower bound — measured.** For any spatial path joining corresponding endpoints, the time integral of its brightness residual equals the endpoint intensity difference. Its squared difference lower-bounds the integrated squared residual. On case08's large-motion landmarks, this endpoint term explains only about5.9% at5k and4.4% at40k of the residual energy measured on straight spatial paths. Endpoint mismatch is therefore insufficient to explain most of that diagnostic residual. This does not prove which intermediate spatial path or parameter path would be best. On the smaller cases the fraction is larger, so it is not a universal statement about lung image photometry.

## What the later controls distinguish

**Coarse-to-fine needs a reliable intermediate map, not just a longer sharp fit.** A two-stage Gaussian fit improves case 08 from 8.48 to 6.39 mm, while a raw-image cascade gives 8.40 mm and a 40k sharp prefix followed by refinement gives 7.20 mm. A three-stage Gaussian coarse/medium/raw cascade reaches 5.34 mm on that case and 1.656 mm on DIR-Lab quick4, with 5k total updates. Brain transfer remains poor, including a folding increase on one pair. These are useful lung results, not a shared-method win. The cost-matched two-stage 2k coarse + 3k raw control reaches 6.10 mm, versus 5.34 mm with the intermediate scale; this supports a scale contribution beyond total update count. A shorter 1k prefix followed by 4k raw updates worsens all three quick4 means, so insufficient fine-stage time alone does not explain the regressions. The quality of the first inferred warp also matters.

**A Laplacian representation could preserve motion while adding detail — first additive proof is insufficient.** The continuous Gaussian curriculum retains OASIS accuracy much better than restarting the pair, but loses much of the large-motion gain seen with frozen map composition. A representation with a retained coarse component and a newly learned high-frequency residual could preserve the useful anatomical path as fine structure appears. The test must distinguish endpoint sharpening from improved temporal transport. Simply summing bands before the same velocity solve could let a strong, inconsistent fine derivative overwhelm the coarse signal; recording each band's contribution to the normal equations is essential before choosing a combination. The first frozen Gaussian512 + trainable fine512 proof gives 7.04 mm on case08 and 0.79640 Dice on OASIS4 at about 169/153 s charged full cost, losing to the map cascade and shared brain recipe. The useful coarse trajectory is not automatically protected when its derivatives are summed with a new fine component. Do not assume that naming a pyramid resolves this interaction.

**More complete spatial observations can reduce noisy motion gradients.** At the same frozen model and crop, three rotated orthogonal pairs reduce field deviation and registration-gradient variation considerably more than two pairs. An independent three-pair control shows that the gain is partly geometric, beyond the extra two sites. Fixed coordinate-axis pairs have zero redraw variance but retain a larger field bias. Their reproducibility is therefore insufficient evidence of accuracy. Three pairs also raise actual training cost substantially. A rotated tetrahedron uses four sites, like the original two pairs, while giving an isotropic second moment in all three dimensions. Its frozen variance reduction is smaller but costs no extra derivative evaluations; a training comparison is warranted. Keep complete cell pooling and live gradients throughout.

**Sampling precision has a small real benefit even without clearing the shared threshold.** With 16k endpoint points, the bounded residual proposal gives DIR TRE 1.840 and abdomen Dice 0.42835, versus 1.882 and 0.42227 for its matched uniform-proposal control. OASIS slightly prefers uniform (0.82436 versus 0.82369). The proposal is improving something beyond simply reducing point count, but cached coarse predictions may locate fine residuals poorly. A future change should first measure correlation between cached and exact sampling scores at early and late checkpoints. Inverse-probability weighting preserves the full-crop population target; finite-sample normalized NCC ratios are not claimed to give unbiased gradients.

**Spend endpoint samples when the gradients need them — queued test.** The early 8k endpoint gradient still has a useful signal-to-noise ratio; late fine fitting is much noisier and benefits from 32k. A fixed 8k→16k→32k curriculum could save early matrix work while preserving the final 3k updates at full sample count. This differs from reducing the batch throughout training. The implementation consumes the same native 32k random streams and takes fixed prefixes, preserving crop choices, registration particles, probe directions and times. Optimizer state and the 5k schedule continue through the transitions. Test both accuracy and actual compilation/graph-transition overhead before treating the saved FLOPs as saved pipeline time.

**Spatial sensitivity mixes motion and image texture.** The directional parameter derivative is `q = J_theta f · delta_theta`; differentiating in coordinates gives the existing mixed derivative `m`. Large values of `m` do not directly identify large displacement. On case 08, its magnitude correlates negatively with motion in raw 5k/40k models and positively in the blurred prefixes. In a transported image, spatial curvature and spatial velocity variation both contribute. Exact synthetic affine transport validates the differentiated identity. Sparse landmark-derived strain explains less than 1% of the measured raw case-08 mixed residual energy at the tested neighbourhood scales. This does not support changing the method to a strain correction as the leading fix; no such objective was implemented.

**NCC-equivalent endpoint gauges can change registration — measured, post-hoc fix failed.** Equalizing the endpoints' global moments, or calibrating them to observed intensities, leaves endpoint Pearson NCC unchanged to about 1e-7 but worsens most tested registration scores. Some image-affine calibrations cause large losses, including case-01 TRE 1.05→2.20 mm. The temporal path contains information that endpoint NCC alone does not specify. This makes the coupled learned gauge part of registration, rather than an interchangeable presentation choice. Independent neuron permutations are another possible endpoint symmetry, but optimizing a discrete alignment is a low priority without evidence that the shared initialization lost its correspondence. Fourier-based feature initialization remains untested; a whole-image Fourier translation probe instead finds that stationary anatomy dominates case 08's coarse NCC (about 0.985 at identity despite 15 mm initial landmark TRE).

**Temporal recovery sampling is a useful speed lead.** The learned path may vary smoothly enough in time that 16 velocity evaluations are unnecessary. Holding the total spatial RK2 integration work at 64 steps, four evaluations give about 6 s recovery on 14 frozen cases with small score changes and converged PCG; eight evaluations are closer still. This tests temporal quadrature, separately from increasing spatial integration substeps on the same fields, which did not fix folding. Validate the final recipe and full cohort before selecting a lower fixed evaluation count. This is a fixed sampling choice within the retained PCG+RK2 recovery, not an adaptive solver.

**Folding needs a flow-versus-map diagnosis — localization completed.** More RK2 substeps do not remove the worst abdomen folds. Frozen localization finds 89–92% of folds in the central 90% image box on the two examined abdomen pairs, and all folds central on the examined OASIS pairs. Abdomen folds lie outside evaluated organ labels, while 38–47% of the brain folds lie inside labels. The Gaussian cascade adds central folds at every stage. These observations do not support an image-edge particle-coverage fix as the leading explanation. A continuous velocity flow can strongly compress anatomy while the finite-difference Jacobian of its sampled map becomes negative. Compare the coordinate Jacobian of sparse integrated particles at folded grid sites against the official sampled-map Jacobian, then refine only the integration at those sites. Positive particle Jacobians with persistent negative map differences would support spatial underresolution/compression; negative particle Jacobians that disappear with finer steps would implicate the integration. Keep whole-grid folding visible and labels evaluation-only.

**Compilation changes the economics of adaptive point budgets — measured.** The 8k→16k→32k endpoint schedule preserves most quick4 quality, but three static point shapes add enough compilation and graph-transition work to increase whole-case time. Reduced endpoint FLOPs alone are not a useful optimization on short per-pair training. A future implementation would need one reusable shape or amortized compilation, and should demonstrate the payoff in actual dispatch-to-output time before more schedules are trained.

**Wider and shallower can be cheaper over the complete fit — in progress.** A 768-wide, three-sine-layer pair has about 50% more parameters than the 512-wide, four-layer reference, yet at 3k rather than 5k updates it uses about 10% less total dense forward arithmetic. It also composes fewer nonlinear layers along the interpolated parameter path. Compare against both the 5k reference and a 512/four-layer/3k control, using complete recovery cost and all three quick4 cohorts. An accuracy gain at equal update count distinguishes a useful representation change from merely compressing the schedule; actual wall time determines whether it is deployable. This experiment changes width and depth together, so it is not a pure capacity attribution.

**Temporal coverage can improve gradient estimates without more evaluations — full-cohort comparison completed.** With crop, queries and particles fixed, independent early/late times produce substantial auxiliary-gradient variation. Antithetic pairing increases variation on the three examined checkpoints; a fixed midpoint rule has no redraw noise but a large discrepancy from a denser temporal reference. Scrambled two-dimensional Sobol points preserve the original joint uniform time distribution and cover it more evenly over a short prefix. Two 16-draw estimates per checkpoint reduce mean-gradient error against an 8×8 reference on DIR 03/08 and OASIS pair 1. Test actual training because crops change and model parameters move between updates; frozen quadrature alone cannot guarantee a benefit. The full GW/orthogonal/Sobol pass preserves all spatial streams and reaches DIR 2.861/2.970 mm, OASIS 0.813490 Dice and abdomen 0.436847 Dice, retaining the GW organ gain while reducing its largest folding tail. The improvement is not universal: OASIS pair changes split 9 improving/10 worsening, and abdomen pair-3 folding remains worse than the original reference. Its main value is a principled quadrature improvement with modest observed transfer, not evidence that all gradient noise should be removed.

**Strong compression can fold a sampled map even with positive particle-flow Jacobians — measured locally.** The directly integrated coordinate Jacobian is positive at all 64 sampled fold sites in each examined brain/abdomen case, including after finer RK2 integration. Original-step particle positions match the dense map. The abdominal median determinant is about 0.0005, indicating severe compression. This points to spatially underresolved/compressed maps in these examples, not a reason to discount the official folding metric. A fixed stronger PCG regularity control tests whether reducing compression preserves useful motion; a later representation study could test whether coarse-to-fine components create compression that survives their composition. Do not introduce evaluation-label gates or claim global flow positivity from sparse probes.

**Update budget and phase travel are different controls — in progress.** Compressing OneCycle from 5k to 3k at the same peak removes about 40% of the LR integral. The resulting brain loss can reflect insufficient optimization travel as well as fewer sampled observations. A 5/3 peak-LR control approximately matches the integral while keeping the same schedule fractions and decay, and should be judged by measured phase changes, instability/clipping, endpoint fidelity and registration. Adam preconditioning and stochastic gradients prevent an equivalence claim. Success would support a faster recipe; failure would direct attention to the number/quality of observations rather than simply increasing per-update movement.

**A zero residual need not have zero hidden gradients — algebra and full VJPs verified.** The first additive coarse/fine proof improves case-08 TRE to 7.04 mm at a charged 5k-update budget, but costs about 169 s and remains below its map-cascade control. Its zero output head preserves the initial coarse path while initially blocking fine hidden-layer gradients. A narrower coarse SIREN could retain smooth anatomy cheaply. Widen its hidden representation into paired common/difference channels. A hidden block `[C+R, C-R; C-R, C+R]/2` preserves the learned common feature under identical paired activations, while a SIREN-scaled random `R` transmits antisymmetric gradients. Opposite scalar-head weights cancel the initial residual image. Using the same construction at both endpoints also preserves all intermediate images and their space/time derivatives. Independent residual parameters nevertheless receive nonzero gradients in every hidden layer. A plain zero-head control has zero hidden gradients. CPU checks pass through four sine layers, including complete loss VJPs and two-component restore. The completed two-case comparison reuses the identical saved smaller coarse pair between head modes. The zero-head control wins: case08 8.192 versus 8.321 mm and OASIS4 0.79730 versus 0.79028 Dice. Nonzero initial hidden gradients alone are not a demonstrated convergence fix. The smaller prefix alone gives 8.662 mm / 0.63996 Dice, so its quality is also limiting. Fine learning reduces case08 large-motion q/target correlation from the frozen coarse component's 0.9251 to 0.8892 (zero) or 0.8595 (opposite head). A fixed coarse image component does not fix the transport of the summed image; the fine derivatives change the normal equations. Retain the function-preserving construction as a tool, not a selected method.

**Separate a bad temporal signal from recovery attenuation — synthetic oracle probe.** Retain a trained model's gradient field, synthesize `q = -g dot u` for known 20 mm velocity modes, and solve the unchanged global brightness PCG. On the initial 64-axis diagnostic grid, case-08 raw gradients recover a constant mode with amplitude ratio 0.997 and a broad cosine mode with ratio 0.977; a finer mode falls to about 0.768. The Gaussian model attenuates the fine mode much more. These controls show that the solver can express large coherent motion when the forcing is consistent, while spatial observability and regularization still affect detail. The 128-axis GPU confirmation gives case-08 constant/broad/fine amplitude ratios 0.9968/0.9785/0.7796, and landmark velocity errors 0.020/0.050/0.953 mm. The Gaussian-only fine mode remains attenuated to 0.4820. These are instantaneous synthetic-forcing checks, not integrated TRE or full-resolution registration. No model or recovery objective is changed, and no deformation field is saved.

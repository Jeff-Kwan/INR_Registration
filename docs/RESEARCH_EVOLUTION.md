# Research evolution

The current method consolidates paired SIRENs, separate MSE or global NCC/moment endpoint reconstruction, full-gradient shared-grid PD-LK, box/GW-NCC registration, and PCG+RK2 recovery. Zero loss weights support future ablations. Other experimental implementations have been removed; their documentation remains archived.

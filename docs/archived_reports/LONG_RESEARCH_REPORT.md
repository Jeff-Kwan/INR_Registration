## What the mechanism probes establish

**Endpoint fidelity does not specify a useful interpolation path.** A shared reconstruction prefix improves endpoint-window NCC yet worsens registration and temporal transport alignment. The independent reconstruction-prefix control also loses, showing that delaying joint registration itself matters. Post-hoc endpoint-affine calibrations can leave NCC unchanged to about 1e-7 while worsening registration substantially. The learned path and its intensity gauge therefore contain information that endpoint correlation alone does not constrain.

**The current recipe converges faster and still benefits from long training.** Continuous case-03 training improves forward/reverse TRE from 1.666/1.692 mm at 5k to 1.330/1.372 mm at 40k. Its large-motion temporal-target correlation reaches 0.9933. Case 08 improves from 8.180/8.529 to 7.029/7.432 mm, but its ≥10 mm-motion landmarks still have 10.440 mm continuous error. Small-motion error is best at 10k and worsens thereafter. The exact first-5k OneCycle trajectory is retained, then optimizer/RNG state continues at terminal LR; this is not a schedule stretched to 40k. All 18 new milestone recoveries and fidelity/path probes are saved.

**The global solver can express large coherent motion when the forcing is consistent.** A frozen oracle probe replaces the learned temporal derivative by `q = −g·u_known` for a 20 mm motion and solves the unchanged global brightness-flow system. At cap 128, case-08 GW5k recovers constant/broad spatial modes with amplitudes 0.9968/0.9785 and landmark velocity errors 0.020/0.050 mm. A faster-varying mode is attenuated to 0.7796, with 0.953 mm error. The 40k checkpoint slightly improves that mode; Gaussian-only features recover the fine mode poorly (0.4820 amplitude, 5.344 mm error). All 12 solves converge, and cap-64 CPU checks agree with the distinction. These are instantaneous known-forcing diagnostics, not integrated oracle TRE. They show that the large actual gap is not explained by the global operator's inability to represent a coherent displacement of that size. Learning a consistent `q` and retaining enough fine spatial information remain separate problems.

**The complete lung cohort has a hard-case tail.** All-ten-case frozen probes raise the ≥10 mm pooled q/target slope from 0.9153 to 0.9317 and median within-case correlation from 0.9680 to 0.9731 for the Sobol candidate. Case 7 improves correlation 0.8379→0.8829, while case 8 stays 0.8655→0.8656 and its slope falls 0.7494→0.7363. A cohort median therefore conceals the unresolved case-08 mechanism. These are straight known landmark paths, not model trajectories; residual magnitudes also depend on learned brightness scale. The probes are complete for all 30 original/Sobol/4k checkpoints. The actual all-case recovered errors confirm persistent under-displacement: among 1,103 ≥10 mm landmarks, the Sobol candidate has continuous forward/reverse TRE 5.069/5.261 mm, with signed along-motion errors −4.244/−4.446 mm. For 285 <2 mm landmarks the errors are 1.027/1.066 mm. The fast 4k candidate worsens every pooled motion bin, reaching 5.215/5.436 mm for ≥10 mm and 1.052/1.109 mm for <2 mm. All 20 frozen case replays preserve the saved rounded/continuous score parity guard. These continuous motion-bin values are distinct from the rounded cohort TRE in the main table.

**Coarse evidence changes capture range.** In case 08, a raw 2k+2k map cascade gives 8.40 mm TRE; a Gaussian 2k prefix plus 2k sharp refinement gives 6.39 mm. A three-stage coarse/medium/raw 5k cascade reaches 5.34 mm. A matched two-stage 2k+3k control gives 6.10 mm, supporting a useful intermediate scale beyond the extra update budget. Yet the three-stage brain Dice falls to 0.80555, with more folds and especially poor small-label transfer. A continuous Gaussian curriculum preserves fine anatomy better but loses most of the large-motion benefit. The issue is retaining a useful coarse transport path while learning detail, not simply adding more sharp updates.

**Preserving a coarse image component does not preserve its recovered motion.** A matched late test freezes the same width-256/2k Gaussian prefix and fits a width-512/3k fine residual. The prefix alone gives case08 8.662 mm and OASIS4 0.63996 Dice. A zero-head residual reaches 8.192 mm / 0.79730; an exactly cancelling opposite-head initialization, with nonzero gradients in every fine hidden layer from the outset, reaches 8.321 mm / 0.79028. Both preserve the entire initial coarse image/time path to below 6e-7, and both use bitwise-identical frozen coarse weights. The zero-head control wins both cases. The extra initial hidden-gradient flow is therefore not a demonstrated convergence fix.

On case08's ≥10 mm paths, the coarse component has q/target correlation 0.9251 and slope 0.8721. Adding learned fine detail changes these to 0.8595/0.7323 for opposite heads and 0.8892/0.7900 for zero heads. Even an unchanged coarse component can lose its influence in the sum: the fine gradients and temporal derivative alter the normal equations and the resulting flow. This supports preserving transport while learning detail as a research problem, rather than equating a frozen anatomical component with a frozen anatomical deformation. These are two mechanism cases; no shared performance gain is claimed.

**Spatial parameter sensitivity mixes motion and texture.** The existing temporal derivative is `q = J_theta f · delta_theta`; its spatial derivative `m` already enters PD-LK. Large `m` is not automatically useful deformation. Its magnitude correlates negatively with landmark motion in raw case-08 fits and positively in Gaussian prefixes. Sparse landmark-derived strain explains under 1% of the tested mixed-residual energy, so it does not support introducing a strain/covariant correction. Spatially averaging frozen raw derivatives at roughly 8 mm improves large-motion q/target alignment, but averaging derivatives before forming normal equations loses much of the local information and does not improve the local velocity diagnostic. A better temporal correlation is not sufficient evidence of a better velocity estimator.

**Gradient quality has identifiable sampling components.** With one crop held fixed, query directions produce far more registration-gradient variation than resampling particles. Two orthogonal pairs reduce frozen quadrature variation at unchanged four-site cost. Three orthogonal pairs reduce it further but cost more and do not transfer consistently. Fixed axes have zero redraw variance but retain bias. A tetrahedral four-site rule gives a modest frozen improvement without a clear shared training win. Keep the same complete-cell solve and live gradients.

**Temporal coverage has a different interaction with optimization.** Independent early/late times also produce substantial variation. Antithetic times increase it on the three examined models; fixed midpoint times eliminate redraw variation but disagree with denser time quadrature. Scrambled two-dimensional Sobol points reduce 16-draw averaged-gradient error against an 8×8 frozen reference from about 0.541→0.341 on DIR03, 0.167→0.045 on DIR08 and 0.137→0.045 on OASIS1. This is a frozen-model result. Crops and parameters change during training, so the complete training/cohort comparison determines its value. No extra training seeds are used.

**The sampled-map folding problem is often compression, rather than insufficient RK2 substeps.** Increasing spatial substeps on the same fields barely changes the measured folds. In two abdomen examples, 89–92% of folds are central and outside evaluated organ labels. All folds in the examined brain cases are central; 38–47% are inside labels. At 64 sampled official fold sites in each of one brain and one abdomen case, the directly integrated particle-flow Jacobian is positive and reproduces the saved map positions. The abdominal median determinant is only about 0.0005. Finer integration keeps those particle Jacobians positive. These observations support severe compression and spatially sampled map differences in those examples; they do not establish global positivity or excuse negative official Jacobians. Stronger PCG regularity reduces folding but also changes alignment, which requires the full metric comparison.

The temporal-sampling fold audit keeps 64 total RK2 substeps and varies only four/eight/sixteen velocity evaluations. In GW abdomen pair 3, primary folding is 1.059/1.099/1.140%; pair 17 is 0.862/0.872/1.009%. The box control is nonmonotone. Additional temporal evaluations therefore do not reliably remove the sampled-map fold tail. Reverse maps also fold: at eight evaluations, GW pairs 3/17 have 0.573/0.651% reverse whole-interior folding, and 0.0296/0.2627% within evaluation-label foreground. Forward background localization cannot be assumed to describe the reverse direction. The leading Sobol candidate also completes that check: pairs 3/17 have 0.625/0.608% reverse whole-interior folds and 0.0091/0.0929% within labeled foreground. The issue remains present in the selected candidate. The fast recipe also has reverse folds on its shared pair-3 stress case and its worst primary-fold pair 11: reverse whole-interior fractions are 0.627/0.689%, with 0.0023/0.0573% within labeled foreground. These are targeted reverse-map checks, not a new full reverse-cohort score.

## Speed and model-size findings

The retained implementation captures sampling, the complete full-gradient objective, backward, clipping and fused AdamW. It uses accurate FP32 LU through `solve_ex` with asynchronous status checking, and preserves every derivative/solve/smoothing/trajectory gradient. Highest FP32 remains the retained numerical mode. Original per-call CUDA RNG replay is checked bitwise for masked/unmasked sampling. Adjacent cases with equal geometry/config reuse compiled code while creating fresh parameters and optimizer state. Four real-input 100-update fits check code reuse and a geometry change; repeated fits introduce no new Dynamo graph and retain close parameter trajectories.

The complete captured step is about 15–16 ms, versus about 25 ms in its matched earlier boundary test. Solver replacement alone did not help: it enabled graph capture, and fusion/capture supplied the gain. Compensated TF32/BF16 kernels and a cuBLASLt preference check lost on measured layouts; no such kernel is promoted. High matmul mode accelerates warm fitting but changes gradients, and cold compilation on varied lung shapes can hide its benefit. Do not equate a faster isolated GEMM with a faster complete registration.

Fixed temporal recovery sampling is useful independently of spatial RK2 refinement. Eight rather than sixteen velocity evaluations, keeping 64 total substeps, gives small score changes on original and GW frozen checkpoints with converged PCG. A cap of 256 leaves OASIS and abdomen grids unchanged and shortens recovery on large lungs while preserving original-voxel units and original evaluation geometry. Both are declared sampling approximations, not adaptive/enhanced recovery.

The 8k→16k→32k endpoint point curriculum preserves much of the quality but introduces multiple static shapes and compiler/graph-transition costs. Warm repeated geometries can benefit, while changing lung shapes lose. Width 768/three layers/3k has about 50% more parameters and 10% less nominal total dense arithmetic than 512/four layers/5k, yet is slower in the actual kernels and loses abdomen accuracy. Smaller models likewise failed to retain the shared quality in earlier controls.

A 3k width-512 fit is faster, but OASIS quick4 Dice falls to 0.81844 from about 0.827 at 5k. Raising peak LR by 5/3 approximately matches the schedule's LR integral and does not recover shared registration quality: accurate/high numerical variants give DIR quick4 2.045/2.005 mm and abdomen 0.41295/0.40489 Dice. The high variant can run below a minute, but it is not selected as a well-rounded replacement. Frozen phase diagnostics separate direct parameter displacement from upstream feature changes; neither is cumulative optimizer path length.

For context, the default width-256/four-layer pair has 397,314 parameters; the width-512 pair has 1,581,058. Mean prepared volumes contain 20,152,320 voxels in DIR-Lab, 6,881,280 in OASIS and 7,864,320 in abdomen. The largest lung has 35,651,584 voxels. These counts contextualize fitting cost and normalized spatial bandwidth, but do not imply one parameter is required per voxel or that capacity alone explains transport error.

## Stretch targets and interpretation

No validated recipe reaches all requested targets together. The leading shared accuracy candidate reaches 0.81349 OASIS Dice versus 0.85, 0.43685 abdomen Dice versus 0.50, and 2.861/2.970 mm DIR-Lab TRE versus 1.00 mm in each direction. Its 93.89-second mean is below two minutes but above the one-minute stretch target. The separate 4k speed frontier meets the pooled one-minute target at 55.01 seconds, but loses OASIS Dice and HD95; it therefore does not satisfy the joint requirement of retaining all accuracy metrics. A faster pipeline does not erase an accuracy loss or a folding increase. These are development results from one seed, not a statistical robustness claim.

## Promising work left open

The additive representation and active-gradient cancellation initialization are verified mechanism prototypes, but their completed matched two-case tests do not justify promotion. The smaller prefix is weak before refinement; adding detail still degrades its temporal transport coherence. Both components are restored explicitly from their own model/state metadata. Historical prefix fitting time is charged when reused, and cold/warm compiler histories make the proof timings unsuitable as a matched speed claim.

The most useful next decisions should follow transport coherence, spatial observability, compression and time to quality. A sharper endpoint, more hidden-feature movement, lower redraw variance or fewer nominal FLOPs is useful only when the corresponding complete registration improves. Preserve the full-cohort fallback and test changes against organ/motion strata and folding tails.

## Mechanisms to pursue next

1. **Protect coarse transport while learning detail.** Coarse images establish a useful capture range, while sharp residuals can change the temporal derivative of the summed representation. First measure whether a refinement preserves the coarse q/target alignment and map amplitude, then compare fine endpoint fidelity at the same full runtime. The additive proof and its matched initialization control identify whether inactive hidden gradients are actually the limiting factor; more initial gradient alone is not a success criterion.
2. **Explain and control compression in both directions.** Time refinement does not reliably repair the fold tail, and foreground/background localization differs by map direction. Use the existing direct-particle versus sampled-map Jacobian check to identify the deformation scales being compressed. A training change should reduce those tails while retaining organ alignment, rather than hiding them through masks or shifted Jacobian metrics. Simply increasing the global PCG prior already loses useful alignment.
3. **Use complete time-to-quality measurements.** Capture and recovery sampling removed large costs. Dense SIREN arithmetic and the required number of useful updates now dominate. Wider/shallow arithmetic estimates, a matched LR integral and faster isolated GEMMs did not predict full registration wins. A new numerical or scheduling change needs full-gradient checks and complete-pipeline accuracy/folding comparisons; the 4k frontier provides the practical baseline for the remaining one-minute gap.
